    How the matching works

    Every 100 m cell is described by 20 numbers about its surroundings, never the cell itself: shares of tree, crop, rangeland, bare, water and built-up land within 250 and 500 m; distances to built-up land, major roads, any road and water; road density; elevation and slope. Each number is standardised, and two cells are compared by the straight-line (Euclidean) distance between their 20 numbers.

    Similarity rescales that distance: 100 % means identical surroundings, 0 % means as different as the 1 % least similar usable cells.

    Only usable land is searched (not built-up, forest, water or protected land). The S1–S3 badge is the cell's class on the suitability map. Data: land cover 2023, OpenStreetMap 2026-10-01. The same calculation as src/similarity/query.py, run in your browser.
