Where can new urban development go in Bengaluru South?

A land suitability analysis built in Python from open land-cover, terrain and OpenStreetMap data. Every method was tested by computing it with data from 2018 only and checking it against the land that was actually built on by 2022 and 2023.

How it works

1

Classify the land

K-Means clustering of 100 m cells on land-cover shares and terrain gives built-up, forest and usable land (96.6 % agreement with the source map).

2

Describe surroundings

Every cell gets a 20-number profile of its surroundings: land cover within 250 and 500 m, distances to built-up land, roads and water, road density and terrain. Never the cell's own land cover.

3

Learn from where building happened

Similarity to existing buildings and a random forest trained on growth between 2018 and 2021 are combined into one score, graded S1–S3.

4

Test against real growth

Scores use 2018 data only and are checked on land built by 2022/23, with out-of-fold scoring and simple baselines, so the numbers are honest.
